## Capture
# Relationship Compatibility Discussion

## How to use this

For each question, both partners answer:

- **My answer:**
    
- **How important is this to me?** Low / Medium / High
    
- **Is this a preference or a deal breaker?**
    
- **What compromise would feel fair?**
    

---

## 1. Relationship Goals

1. What kind of relationship are we trying to build?
    
2. Do we both want marriage? If yes, what general timeline feels right?
    
3. Do we want children? How many, and when?
    
4. What would make either of us feel that the relationship is no longer progressing?
    
5. What does commitment mean to each of us?
    

---

## ~~2. Values and Beliefs~~

1. ~~What personal values are most important to us?~~
    
2. ~~How important are religion and spiritual practices?~~
    
3. ~~Are there beliefs or values we cannot compromise on?~~
    
4. ~~How should we handle major disagreements about morals, politics, or religion?~~
    
5. What behaviours would make us lose respect for a partner?
    

---

## 3. Trust and Loyalty

1. What behaviours do we consider cheating?
    
2. Is flirting with other people acceptable? Where is the boundary?
    
3. What boundaries should we have with former partners?
    
4. Are we comfortable with each other having close friends of the opposite sex?
    
5. What level of privacy should we have regarding phones, messages, and social media?
    
6. What would we need to rebuild trust after dishonesty?
    

---

## 4. Communication

1. How often do we expect to communicate when we are apart?
    
2. Do we prefer discussing problems immediately or after taking time to calm down?
    
3. What makes us feel ignored or unheard?
    
4. How should we bring up complaints without attacking each other?
    
5. Are there words, tones, or behaviours that should never be used during an argument?
    
6. How do we want our partner to respond when we are stressed?
    

---

## 5. Conflict and Apologies

1. What usually happens when each of us becomes angry?
    
2. Is taking space during conflict acceptable? For how long?
    
3. What does a sincere apology look like to us?
    
4. How should we handle repeated problems?
    
5. What behaviours during conflict would be immediate deal breakers?
    
6. When should we ask someone else, such as a counsellor, for help?
    

---

## 6. Emotional Needs

1. What makes each of us feel loved and appreciated?
    
2. How much reassurance do we need?
    
3. How much quality time feels sufficient?
    
4. How comfortable are we discussing emotions and vulnerabilities?
    
5. What makes us feel emotionally neglected?
    
6. How should we support each other during difficult periods?
    

---

## 7. Affection and Sexual Compatibility

1. How important are physical affection and sex to each of us?
    
2. How often would we ideally want physical intimacy?
    
3. How should we communicate when one person is not interested?
    
4. What are our boundaries regarding consent and sexual activities?
    
5. Are there sexual expectations that we need to discuss honestly?
    
6. What should we do when our levels of desire are different?
    
7. What would make either person feel pressured or rejected?
    

---

## 8. Money and Career

1. ~~How do we currently manage money?~~
    
2. ~~Are we comfortable discussing income, savings, debt, and spending?~~
    
3. ~~Should finances eventually be separate, combined, or a mixture?~~
    
4. What spending decisions should require discussion?
    
5. How important are career ambition and financial stability?
    
6. ~~How should expenses be divided if our incomes are different?~~

7. ~~What financial behaviours would be deal breakers?~~
    
8. Would either of us relocate or change careers for the relationship?
    

---

## 9. Household Responsibilities

1. How should chores be divided?
    
2. What level of cleanliness do we expect at home?
    
3. Who should handle planning, appointments, bills, and other mental tasks?
    
4. How should responsibilities change when one person is busier?
    
5. What would make the division of work feel unfair?
    
6. How should childcare responsibilities be divided in the future?
    

---

## 10. Family and Social Life

1. How involved should our families be in our relationship?
    
2. How often do we expect to visit or communicate with family?
    
3. How should we handle family members who interfere with the relationship?
    
4. Should family problems remain private or be discussed with relatives?
    
5. How important is approval from parents or family?
    
6. How much time should we spend with friends separately and together?
    
7. What boundaries should exist between our partner and our family?
    

---

## 11. Independence and Personal Space

1. How much alone time does each of us need?
    
2. Are separate hobbies, trips, or social activities acceptable?
    
3. What behaviour feels controlling to us?
    
4. How often do we expect updates about each other’s location or plans?
    
5. What parts of our lives should remain individual?
    
6. How can we maintain independence without making the other person feel excluded?
    

---

## 12. Lifestyle and Health

1. How important are exercise, diet, sleep, and personal hygiene?
    
2. How do we feel about smoking, alcohol, gambling, or recreational drugs?
    
3. What lifestyle habits would be unacceptable?
    
4. How should we respond if one partner develops an addiction?
    
5. Are our preferred social lifestyles compatible?
    
6. How do we feel about frequent travelling, gaming, nightlife, or other time-consuming activities?
    
7. What health information should partners share with each other?
    

---

## 13. Personal Growth

1. What personal weaknesses are we currently trying to improve?
    
2. Are we willing to receive honest feedback from each other?
    
3. How should feedback be communicated?
    
4. What would make us feel unsupported in our growth?
    
5. Can we accept that both people may change over time?
    
6. What happens if one person grows in a very different direction?
    

---

## 14. Individual Deal Breakers

Each partner should complete these sentences separately:

1. I would immediately reconsider the relationship if…
    
2. I could not stay in a relationship involving…
    
3. I need a partner who is willing to…
    
4. I can compromise on…
    
5. I cannot compromise on…
    
6. A behaviour that might seem small but is very important to me is…
    
7. A boundary I need you to understand is…
    
8. Something I am afraid to discuss but believe we should discuss is…
    

---

# Final Comparison

After answering, discuss:

1. Where are our answers already aligned?
    
2. Where are our differences manageable?
    
3. Which differences require a specific compromise?
    
4. Are there any true deal breakers?
    
5. Are we both genuinely comfortable with the proposed compromises?
    
6. What should we revisit in three to six months?
