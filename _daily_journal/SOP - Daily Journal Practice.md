# SOP — Daily Journal Practice

Standard Operating Procedure untuk journaling yang efektif dan consistent.

## ⏰ Timing

### Best Practices
- **Morning (5-10 min)**: Set intention for the day
- **Evening (10-15 min)**: Reflect on the day, capture wins & lessons
- **Optional - Midday (2 min)**: Quick emotion check-in

### Consistency Over Perfection
- Write even if just 2-3 sentences
- Skip if you're overwhelmed (→ then write the next day)
- No "catching up" entries — each day stands alone

---

## 📝 Daily Journal Structure

### 1️⃣ Morning Intention (5 min)
**Purpose**: Set mindset for the day

**Questions to ask:**
- What do I want to accomplish today?
- What's my biggest priority?
- How do I want to feel?
- Any challenges I anticipate?

**Keep it short.** 2-3 sentences is fine.

---

### 2️⃣ Evening Reflection (10-15 min)
**Purpose**: Process the day, extract wisdom

**Questions to ask:**

**Reflection:**
- What actually happened today?
- How did I feel throughout?
- Any surprises or unexpected moments?

**Wins & Gratitude:**
- What went well, even small things?
- Who/what am I grateful for?
- What am I proud of?

**Challenges & Learning:**
- What was difficult?
- What did I learn from it?
- How can I apply this lesson?

**Closing:**
- How am I feeling now?
- Any final thoughts?

## 🎯 Mood & Energy Tracking

Use emojis + numbers for quick mood logging:

**Mood Options:**
- 😊 Good/Happy
- 😐 Neutral/Okay
- 😔 Sad/Down
- 😤 Frustrated/Angry
- 😰 Anxious/Stressed
- 😴 Tired/Exhausted

**Energy (1-10):**
- 10 = Peak energy, unstoppable
- 7-9 = High energy
- 5-6 = Medium/okay
- 3-4 = Low energy
- 1-2 = Exhausted

## 🔄 Weekly Review (Optional but Recommended)

Every Sunday evening (or your preferred day):

1. **Read through the week's entries** (5 min)
2. **Extract 1-2 key lessons** → save to [[3. Resources]] if applicable
3. **Identify patterns** → Mood trends? Energy patterns?
4. **Update monthly summary** with highlights

## 💡 Tips for Consistency

✅ **Do:**
- Write at the same time each day (build habit)
- Keep pen & paper nearby for morning brainstorm
- Use template, but adapt as needed
- Be honest — journal for yourself, not an audience

❌ **Don't:**
- Force entries if you're burnt out
- Write for perfect grammar/prose
- Include only "positive" thoughts — this is for processing
- Skip the evening reflection — it's where the magic happens
