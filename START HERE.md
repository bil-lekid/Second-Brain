## 🔄 Workflow

### Capture Phase
- Quick idea? → [[0. Fleeting|Fleeting Notes]]
- Personal moment? → [[_daily_journal|Daily Journal]]

### Process Phase (Weekly)
- Review Fleeting inbox
- Extract actionable items → [[1. Projects|Projects]] or [[2. Areas|Areas]]
- Extract knowledge → [[Tasks|Resources]]
- Discard or archive

### Reflect Phase (Daily)
- Evening: Fill in [[_daily_journal|daily journal]]
- Weekly: Review the week
- Monthly: Review the month & extract lessons

## ⚡ Pro Tips

✅ **Consistent capture** — Fleeting notes are worthless if not reviewed
✅ **Weekly processing** — Move things out of Fleeting to their home
✅ **Daily reflection** — 10 min journaling = clarity + growth
✅ **Monthly review** — See patterns in how you spend time and energy

❌ **Don't** let Fleeting become a junk drawer
❌ **Don't** skip daily journal — it's not optional, it's therapeutic
❌ **Don't** hoard resources — prune regularly
